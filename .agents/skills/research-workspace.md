name: Research Workspace
description: Automated strategy dossier generation and research logging.

# Goal
Generate detailed, auditable analytical reports for any strategy run or backtested model[cite: 1].

# Report Sections
- **Executive Overview**: Underlying asset, strategy classification, execution timeframes, and validation score[cite: 1].
- **Mechanism Breakdown**: Plain-language explanation of entry rules, exit rules, and edge conditions[cite: 1].
- **Risk Metrics**: Maximum drawdown, historical expectancy, Profit Factor, Sharpe, and Probability of Profit (POP)[cite: 1].
- **Sensitivities**: Parameter variation grids, strike offset sensitivity, and IV shift impact[cite: 1].
- **Trade Distribution**: Return dispersion, average holding duration, Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE), and Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE)[cite: 1].
- **AI Assessment**: Model-generated critiques outlining potential weaknesses, market regime conflicts, and optimization suggestions[cite: 1].
